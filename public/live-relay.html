The host · starts the room · @blkoutuk

Host: your steps

The day before — three things:

1

In Instagram settings, search archive and switch on Save Live to Archive. This is the safety net.

2

Charge the phone. Bring a power bank on Sunday.

3

Turn on Do Not Disturb before you start. One phone call kills a live.

Going live:

4

Tap + then LIVE. You're on.

5

Tap the two-faces button at the bottom of your screen.

6

Tap Invite and pick the other two parks. Their handles are pinned in the group chat. When they accept, they appear on screen next to you.

7

If someone asks to join instead, say yes from the same screen.

Ending — do not rush this bit:

8

Tap End. On the next screen tap Download before anything else. Don't swipe away.

9

Then tap Share and post the replay as a Reel. That's the public copy, done.

Missed the download? Breathe. The Archive copy from step 1 keeps it for 30 days.

The guests · join the room · Burgess & Haggerston

Guest: your steps

1

At 5.55, open Instagram and go to the host's page. Their photo will say LIVE — tap it and watch.

2

An invite pops up on your screen. Tap Accept. You're on screen with everyone.

3

No invite? Tap Request to join in the live. The host will let you in.

4

Mute yourself when it's not your turn — tap your own picture. You're still visible, so point the camera at something good.

5

Signal drops? Close the app, reopen, rejoin the live. If it drops twice: go live on your own account at your time slot and hand over with words. Nobody watching will know the difference.

The recorder · one named person · a phone that is NOT broadcasting

Recorder: your one job

1

Watch the live like a normal viewer and screen-record the whole thing.

2

Afterwards, check the file actually plays. On Wednesday this is the whole point of you.

Sunday: three minutes of talking each. Wednesday: same order, one minute each. Handing over is one line — "That's us. Over to Regent's Park." — then mute.

Three rules — they matter

Never say an exact spot out loud. Each park's location goes to its own sign-ups, not on a live stream.

The collection: Sunday only, one sentence. Nothing about it on Wednesday. On Sunday, whoever's talking says one line — "All three picnics are collecting today for queer people in Accra organising under Ghana's new anti-LGBTQ bill. Give at blkoutuk.com/picnic." Say it once, the same way, and don't add names or detail on air.

At 5.45 on Sunday, say this out loud in your park:

"We're going live at six with the other two picnics. Quarter of an hour, we're on for about three minutes. If you'd rather not be on camera, this end of the field is out of shot."

Questions before Wednesday? Ask in the group chat, or email a human.
