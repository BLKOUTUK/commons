Christmas, together

Joseph Beam Day

Monday 28 December 2026. Let's shape the Christmas holidays together, so no Black queer man has to spend them alone.

For Black queer organisations, groups and venues across the UK

Shape it with us

Why this day

Black men loving Black men

Joseph Beam edited In the Life, the landmark 1986 anthology by Black gay men, and wrote that Black men loving Black men is the revolutionary act. 28 December falls between the anniversaries of his death and his birth.

Christmas is when many of us feel the distance most: from family, from community, from each other. So we're making the holidays a time of gathering, building to a day on Monday 28 December when we host each other, share a meal at Ivor's Table, and open BLKOUT's membership.

In Croydon, a crew of Black queer men is designing the day itself, session by session, through the Choose Joy programme. Now we're inviting other organisations in, so the holidays hold more than one day, in more than one place.

How you could be part of it

Bring what you have

Host a moment

A walk, a meal, a check-in, a film, an online gathering: anything between Christmas Eve and New Year that brings Black queer people together. We'll list it alongside ours.

Bring your people

Invite your members to Joseph Beam Day, and to Ivor's Table, the shared dinner named after Ivor Cummings, who welcomed the Windrush in 1948.

Share a space or a skill

A room, a kitchen, a sound system, a cook, a facilitator, a listening ear. Tell us what you could offer.

Care over the holidays

If you run support or advice, help us make sure anyone finding the season hard knows where to turn.

What we bring: listings on events.blkoutuk.com, the community-owned tools in the Commons (event listings and ticketing), our newsletter and channels, and our life-size BLKOUT Review cover booth.

The plan for the day is final on Wednesday 2 December, and invitations go out from the 3rd, so tell us by Friday 27 November if you can.
