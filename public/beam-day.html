An alternative Black queer Christmas

Joseph Beam Day

Monday 28 December 2026. A Christmas nobody has to edit themselves for, with room at the table for every generation.

Where Stanley Arts, South Norwood SE25 (to be confirmed) · Who Black queer men, and everyone who loves us

Shape it with us

Why this day

A table with room at it

Christmas is the family holiday, and many of us have complicated families. Some of us spend the day at tables where we leave half of ourselves in the hallway with the coats. Others spend it with nobody at all.

Joseph Beam, who edited In the Life, the landmark anthology of Black gay men's writing, wrote that Black men loving Black men is the revolutionary act. 28 December falls between the anniversaries of his death and his birth, in the days after Christmas when the obligations end and the loneliness doesn't. So that Monday, we're setting a table.

It's intergenerational on purpose: younger men, elders, and men who have come here seeking sanctuary, all at the same table. It's centred on Black queer men, and it's open beyond us too: friends, chosen family and anyone who'd otherwise be on their own are welcome. It's also the day BLKOUT's membership opens, because people join what they have already felt.

The shape of the day

What's already in place

  • The TableBlack Men Who Brunch returns: an open, pay-what-you-can table in the Gallery from late morning into the afternoon, with pay-it-forward seats so cost keeps nobody away.
  • The CoverOur life-size BLKOUT Review cover booth, built by the Croydon crew this autumn. Step in and leave with your own cover. Showing your face is always optional.
  • The ListeningThe crew's alternative King's Speech, a Christmas message from Black queer men in Croydon, and their spoken invitations.
  • Ivor's TableAn evening dinner hosted by the crew, each bringing his people. Named after Ivor Cummings, who welcomed the Windrush in 1948. The dinner is already paid for.
  • The GiftEvery guest goes home with a gift: an imprint of the Ivor's Compass journal, sponsored by Portaul.

We've invested in booking the space, the dinner is funded, and we can put resources towards travel so getting there isn't the barrier.

Where you come in

Bring something to the table

We'd love the day to hold more than we can bring on our own. What could you add?

An activity

Arts and crafts, a band or a DJ, a quiz, games, a reading, a dance class: something that gets people talking and nobody has to arrive knowing anyone.

Your people

Reserved places for people you support who'd otherwise be alone, with a welcome buddy on the day. Over-18s.

A gift or a hand

Books, food, beauty, phone data or tickets for the gift packs. Or a cook, a facilitator, a volunteer, a listening ear.

Care over the holidays

Your services on the card of who's open over Christmas, and ours on yours. Or a twin gathering in your own town on the same day.

The Croydon crew finalises the plan on Wednesday 2 December and invitations go out from the 3rd, so tell us by Friday 27 November if you can.
