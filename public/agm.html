BLKOUT · Annual General Meeting

Come and shape membership

Wednesday 25 November7pm–8.30pmOnline

This is the first annual general meeting of BLKOUT as a co-operative, owned by its members. Membership opens to everyone on Joseph Beam Day, 28 December. Before it does, we want to hear from you: what should joining BLKOUT mean, and what do you want from it?

You don't need to be a member to come. Members vote on the formal business; everyone is welcome to take part in the conversation and to speak.

The evening

  1. The year in numbers. Members receive the accounts for our first year.
  2. The audit. A resolution for members.
  3. Electing the board. Every seat is open at a first AGM.
  4. The shape of membership. An open conversation, not a vote, and the main part of the evening.

The formal business takes about 20 minutes. Read the report first if you like: BLKOUT's Year One.
