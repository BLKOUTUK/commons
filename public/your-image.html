BLKOUT.

Photos, video and recordings of you

Your image and voice, your say.

Most consent forms put the catch in small print. We put ours first, in big letters, and explain each one here. Nothing about you is smuggled past you.

When BLKOUT photographs, films or records you, we ask you to sign a short form. These are the six promises at the top of it, in full.

You choose where it goes.

The form lists five places we might use what we recorded:

  • our printed materials, such as journals, posters and exhibitions
  • our websites, including BLKOUT Voices
  • our films and podcasts, including on YouTube and podcast platforms
  • our social media
  • press coverage of BLKOUT, and our reports to funders

You tick the ones you're happy with. Tick nothing and we publish nothing for now: you still get your copies. For five years, if we want to use it somewhere you didn't tick, we ask you first. After that, the archive rules below apply.

Our archive opens after five years.

Black queer life in the UK is made every day, and it deserves a record. So BLKOUT keeps the photos and recordings it makes, as a lasting archive of our lives.

For the first five years after we record you, the archive is private: only BLKOUT's own team can see it, and nothing leaves it for anything you haven't ticked without asking you.

After five years, it can be made public as part of a record of Black queer life in the UK: for history, research and exhibitions. A promise of privacy forever would leave a whole collection locked away, and our history deserves to be seen.

If you'd rather yours stays private for good, tick the box on the form, or email us at any time. We'll keep it private.

You can stop new uses any time.

Changed your mind? Email hello@blkoutuk.com with the event and the photo or clip numbers on your form. You don't have to give a reason.

From then on we stop any new use, and we take down single photos and posts of you from our websites and social media. What we recorded stays in the archive. If you want it kept private for good, say so in the same email.

Finished work stays finished.

A film, a podcast episode or a publication is made of many people's contributions. Once the edit is finished, we won't re-cut it for one person, because that would undo everyone else's part in it. If you want to know whether a piece you're in is finished, ask us.

Copies already printed and given out, or already shown, can't be called back.

Your safety comes first.

For some of us, being seen can cost a home, a job, family or safety. If that's true for you, even in a finished piece, tell us. We'll do what we can: blur you, cut you, or remove you, including from the archive. You don't have to tell us more than you want to.

We edit with care.

We edit video and audio for length and clarity, and we add captions. We never edit to change what you meant.

Also worth knowing

Why we ask if you're 18 or over. This form is for adults. If you're under 18, please don't sign it: talk to us first.

The newsletter. Ticking it is optional and separate. You get BLKOUT's monthly newsletter by email, and every one has a link to unsubscribe.

Credit. You choose how you're credited: your name, a first name, a handle, or no credit at all.

Your form. We keep your signed form with the photos and recordings for as long as we keep them, so we can always show what you agreed. You get your own sealed copy when you sign.

Your data. You can ask what we hold about you at any time. Our privacy policy is at blkoutuk.com/privacy.

BLKOUT is the Black Queer Men's Liberation Collective. We host gatherings, build space for dialogue, and create community-owned technology for the health, wealth and happiness of Black gay, bi and trans men across the UK. Through local chapters, cooperative membership, creative events, shared learning, and digital media, we turn connection into individual well-being and collective power — owned by and accountable to the people we serve.
