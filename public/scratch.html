The quest

Nobody's mapped this yet

Same scroll. Same chat. Same resolutions as last year.

This winter BLKOUT is assembling a crew: eight Black queer men who live, work or play in Croydon, setting out together to find what Black queer joy looks like here, and to bring back the best. Because we deserve it.

Each session we try something new, side by side, and find out what works for us. Then the crew reports back to everyone: this is grounded in our experience; this is what works for us, here. What you find shapes what BLKOUT becomes in 2027.

How it runs

The route

  • Dinner workshops · mostly Wednesday eveningsBuild the booth, write, and record, with dinner every time.
  • Sunday 25 October · morningRage into art with Victor Esses, at Stanley Arts.
  • Taking the booth outThe crew takes the cover booth to an event and puts others on the cover.
  • BLKOUT's first AGM · NovemberBe in the room as members talk about what BLKOUT becomes.
  • Monday 28 December · Joseph Beam DayThe crew welcomes everyone in, and hosts the first Ivor's Table.
  • The debriefThe crew's findings, in their own words, for the whole membership.

We confirm venues and exact dates with the crew before we start. Everything is in or near central Croydon.

Who it's for

We saved a space for you

Black gay, bi and trans men who live, work or play in Croydon. No experience of writing, performing or being photographed needed: just curiosity, and a taste for adventure.

Keeping in touch

Between sessions the crew keeps in touch through its own group on BLKOUTHUB, BLKOUT's community space. If you're not on it yet, we'll help you join.

A few reflections

After each session we'll ask a couple of short questions about how it went. It helps us learn, and it's how our funder hears what the programme meant to you.
