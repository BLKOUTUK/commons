What this is

An invitation, from the ground you stand on

Making Ourselves From Scratch brings eight Black queer men in Croydon to three workshops on one theme: invitation from a place. Each of you chooses a Croydon place that carries our history and writes from it: who you're asking, to what, and why this ground matters.

Black queer men rarely see themselves on a magazine cover. Here you make one. Together we build a life-size cover of BLKOUT Review, BLKOUT's forthcoming magazine, and you photograph each other in it, each under his own headline.

It ends on 28 December, Joseph Beam Day, named for the Black gay writer who wrote that Black men loving Black men is the revolutionary act. You host a dinner for the people you invited, your recording is published, and you take your printed cover home.

How it runs

Choose your place. Find your headline. Make the cover. Host your people.

01

Choose your place

A Croydon place that carries our history. Write a monologue of invitation from it, with writer and performer Derek Aidoo: who you're asking, to what, and why this ground matters.

02

Find your headline

A storytelling workshop with Rob Berkeley: the story you'd tell a stranger, cut to one line. Then work your monologue with Derek until it holds a room, and record it in your own voice.

03

Make the cover

A Saturday afternoon building a life-size magazine cover together. Then you photograph each other in it, each under his own headline.

04

Host your people

Monday 28 December, Joseph Beam Day, in the Living Room at Stanley Arts. Your people at your table, your recording published that day, your cover to take home.

When

Late October to November 2026: two weekday evenings and one Saturday afternoon. We confirm the exact dates with the group before we start.

Where

Croydon Voluntary Action's Waterside Centre, South Norwood. Joseph Beam Day is at Stanley Arts.

Who it's for

Black gay, bi and trans men in Croydon. No experience of writing, performing or being photographed needed.

Keeping in touch

Between sessions the group keeps in touch through its own group on BLKOUTHUB, BLKOUT's community space. If you're not on it yet, we'll help you join.

A few reflections

After each session we'll ask a couple of short questions about how it went. It helps us learn, and it's how our funder hears what the programme meant to you.
